## Call for System Demonstrations – ICAPS 2026

*TL;DR:*
- *propose a demonstration to showcase at ICAPS 26*
- *deadline: March 31st*
- *Full call and details: https://icaps26.icaps-conference.org/calls/demos/*


The **ICAPS 2026 System Demonstrations Program** invites researchers and practitioners in automated planning and scheduling to showcase their working systems in action. This is a unique opportunity to highlight real-world impact, novel capabilities, and innovative applications in front of the planning community.

We welcome any system with strong ties to automated planning or scheduling, including but not limited to:

 - Cutting-edge research prototypes
 - Deployed or in-development applications
 - Tools for teaching or visualizing planning/scheduling
 - Systems powered by large language models, reinforcement learning, or hybrid approaches

🏆 An award will be given for the Best System Demonstration!

### Submission instructions

Submissions consist of a **2-page abstract** (AAAI format) and a no more than **10-minute video demo**, both critical for evaluation. Accepted demos will be featured on the ICAPS website and showcased during the conference.

💡 Submissions should emphasize:

- ✔️ *Robustness* – How does the system handle execution and edge cases?
- ✔️ *Generalization* – Can it adapt to new domains or scenarios?
- ✔️ *Scalability* – How does performance evolve with problem size?
- ✔️ *Deployment Effort* – What’s required to model, train, or adapt the system?

**Full Call and submission details:** https://icaps26.icaps-conference.org/calls/demos/

**Submission page:** https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=icaps26demo
